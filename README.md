# RAMA_BANA — 3D Precision Archery Simulator

Play live: **https://saiqulmodi.github.io/RAMA_BANA/**

## Core Features
- **3D Ballistic Physics & Non-Permanent Zeroing:** Gravity drop, draw-weight velocity (`25–70 lbs`), dual-zone wind shear (`Line Wind` + `Downrange Wind`), and automatic post-shot sight drift requiring active recalibration every arrow.
- **0.002° Optical Micro-Sights & 3.5× Scope:** Dedicated `±0.002°` and `±0.050°` elevation (`Up + / Down −`) and windage (`Left − / Right +`) click adjusters plus scope-scaled drag, keyboard (`WASD`/Arrows), and PS5 DualSense stick aiming.
- **Four Target Disciplines:** Stationary (`1.00×`), Straight Rail (`1.25×`), Pendulum Arc (`1.50×`), and 5-Target Lateral Line `#1–#5` spaced `1.0m` apart (`1.75×`).
- **Rama Astra Power Pills:** `Divya Drishti` (`Key 1` — 65% target slow-mo + wide sweet spot), `Vayu Stambhana` (`Key 2` — 0.0 m/s wind lock for 2 shots), and `Agni Astra` (`Key 3` — 3D solar fireball + shockwave + `2.0×` score).
- **Diagnostics & Multiplayer:** Corner Zoom Spotter card (`cm` offset telemetry), Slow-Motion Bullet/Arrow Cam (`Key B`) & Last-Shot Replay (`Key R`), Ballistic DOPE Rangefinder Card, 2-Player Pass-and-Play mode, and 4 unlockable bows.
